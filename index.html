FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What kind of goal is a good fit?

A well-understood goal that's stalled on capacity, not clarity: replatforms, migrations, new internal products, data consolidation, or AI features headed to production.

Why six months?

Long enough to ship to production, short enough to keep scope fixed: two weeks of planning plus twelve two-week iterations.

What if priorities change mid-sprint?

Your product owner reprioritizes at every iteration planning. If the goal itself changes, we re-plan it with you openly.

How quickly can a team start?

At Nike, Google, and Microsoft, procurement is already done. Elsewhere, we refine the backlog while onboarding runs.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do: mutual NDAs, full IP assignment, and work inside your repositories.

What happens after week 26?

Your team owns it, with docs, runbooks, and a paired handoff. Or we design the next sprint.
